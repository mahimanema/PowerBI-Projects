# E-Commerce Sales & Profitability Analysis using Python

## Project Overview

The E-Commerce Sales & Profitability Analysis project is an end-to-end data analytics solution developed using Python to analyze e-commerce sales data, evaluate business performance, identify customer purchasing behavior, and uncover key profitability drivers. The project focuses on transforming raw transactional data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), feature engineering, statistical analysis, and data visualization.

The analysis examines important business metrics such as monthly sales trends, monthly profit trends, product category and sub-category performance, customer segment contribution, and the relationship between sales and profitability. By analyzing these business dimensions, the project helps identify revenue-generating categories, profitable customer segments, and overall business performance patterns that support strategic decision-making.

The project demonstrates a complete analytics workflow beginning with data preprocessing and ending with business insights and recommendations. Various visualizations were created to communicate analytical findings effectively and simplify complex business information for decision-makers.

---

## Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of transactional data every day, making it difficult to identify revenue trends, profitable products, customer purchasing patterns, and areas requiring business improvement.

Without proper analysis, organizations may struggle to understand which product categories contribute the most to sales, which customer segments generate the highest value, how profitability changes over time, and where operational improvements are needed.

This project addresses these challenges by analyzing historical sales data and presenting meaningful business insights through structured exploratory data analysis and visualization. The findings support data-driven decision-making by helping businesses evaluate sales performance, monitor profitability, identify growth opportunities, and improve overall business strategy.

---

## Project Analysis

### Monthly Sales Analysis

Analyzed monthly sales performance to identify business growth trends, seasonal demand patterns, and revenue fluctuations throughout the year.

### Product Category Analysis

Evaluated sales performance across different product categories to identify the highest revenue-generating categories and their contribution to overall business performance.

### Product Sub-Category Analysis

Performed detailed sub-category analysis to compare product-level performance and identify products contributing significantly to revenue generation.

### Monthly Profit Analysis

Analyzed monthly profit trends to evaluate business profitability over time and understand changes in financial performance.

### Profit by Category

Examined profit generated by each product category to determine the highest and lowest performing business categories.

### Profit by Sub-Category

Analyzed profitability across different product sub-categories to identify products contributing positively and negatively to business performance.

### Customer Segment Analysis

Compared sales and profit across different customer segments to understand purchasing behavior and identify valuable customer groups.

### Sales-to-Profit Ratio Analysis

Evaluated the relationship between sales and profit to understand business efficiency and overall profitability.
