# 📊 Power BI & Data Analytics Projects

Welcome to my Data Analytics portfolio! This repository showcases a collection of end-to-end projects demonstrating my skills in **Power BI, Python, SQL, Machine Learning, Data Visualization, and Business Intelligence**. Each project focuses on solving real-world business problems by transforming raw data into meaningful insights and actionable solutions.

The projects cover descriptive, diagnostic, and predictive analytics across different business domains, including financial risk analysis, digital payment analytics, customer segmentation, and machine learning. They demonstrate the complete analytics workflow—from data preparation and exploratory analysis to interactive dashboard development and predictive modeling.

## 📂 Projects

### 📌 Loan Risk Analysis & Loan Approval Prediction
An end-to-end analytics solution combining **Power BI** and **Machine Learning** to analyze loan applications, evaluate applicant financial strength, identify risk patterns, and predict loan approval using a Random Forest model.

**Tools & Technologies:** Power BI, Python, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, Power Query

---

### 📌 PhonePe: Digital Payment Analytics Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes digital payment transactions across Insurance, Loans, Money Transfer, and Recharge & Bill Payments to monitor transaction performance, identify failure reasons, and evaluate payment trends.

**Tools & Technologies:** Power BI, Power Query, Data Visualization

---

### 📌 Retail Sales Analysis & RFM Customer Segmentation
A customer analytics project that analyzes retail sales data to uncover purchasing behavior and segments customers using the RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) model, enabling targeted marketing and customer retention strategies.

**Tools & Technologies:** Python, SQL, Microsoft Excel, Pandas
